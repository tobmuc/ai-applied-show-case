01 · Feedback IntelligenceContext-rich customer signals

Preserve the voice. Connect it to the product.

Not just positive/negative labels: keep the original language and relate each signal to the feature and operating context.

  • Compare markets with original text and normalized translation together.
  • Connect topic and sentiment to feature, channel, country, platform and version.
  • Make outputs reviewable with reference examples, confidence checks and quality review.

02 · Veezoo app analyticsAsynchronous self-service

Explore app usage without the ad-hoc queue.

The target: a Veezoo-based portal where teams investigate governed app and feature metrics themselves, instead of repeatedly queueing one-off requests with partners or service providers.

  • Use presentation-ready visualizations to report app and feature adoption to management.
  • Monitor usage after feature launches; test hypotheses and explore potential correlations.
  • Move from a question to a chart and take results into existing work with CSV, Excel and editable PowerPoint.

Feedback classification · scenario, not a forecast

Illustrative potential: ~€249k gross labor-cost difference / year

Based on the stated daily volume and token estimate plus explicit manual-work assumptions. This is a gross scenario estimate—not measured, guaranteed or net savings.

Inputs to validate with operating data
See assumptions, break-even threshold and calendar-day variant

Scenario uses 1.5 minutes of manual handling per item, €40 fully loaded labor cost/hour and 250 processing days/year. For a gross difference above €200k, manual handling must average more than ~1.21 minutes/item under those assumptions. If 1,000 items are processed on all 365 calendar days, the same inputs yield ~€363,540/year gross (€365,000 labor minus €1,460 tokens). Implementation, human review, infrastructure and ongoing operations are excluded; released capacity is not automatically cash savings.
