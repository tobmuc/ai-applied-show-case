One connected solution

From raw signals
to shared action.

A clear operating model connects customer feedback with governed analytics, so teams can move from a customer signal to a measurable follow-up.

STEP 01

Capture

Bring together app reviews, in-app comments, CSAT responses and support conversations.

STEP 02

Normalize

Translate where needed and attach consistent channel, platform, version and product metadata.

STEP 03

Understand

Use AI to label topics and sentiment, map feedback to features, and validate results against examples.

STEP 04

Explore

Query shared business metrics in natural language, backed by a defined data model and warehouse.

STEP 05

Share & act

Visualize trends, distribute role-appropriate views and export results for follow-up.

Explicit solution benefits

Why this approach
creates value.

The value comes from connecting the workflow end to end—not from a model alone. These are the intended benefits of the showcased solution design.

🗂️

One view of customer voice

Combine feedback from different channels with product, platform, country and version context instead of reviewing isolated fragments.

🌍

Multilingual reach

Preserve original-language feedback while making translated content easier to compare across markets and languages.

⚡

Faster insight cycles

Automate repetitive triage and let teams explore defined business metrics with natural-language questions.

🧭

Consistent product context

Map customer language to shared feature and KPI definitions so trends are easier to compare and prioritize.

✅

More trustworthy outputs

Use ground-truth examples, quality checks, access controls and traceable metadata to make results easier to review.

📤

Insights people can use

Give product, support and leadership views that fit their needs, with charts and practical CSV, Excel and PowerPoint exports.

My applied skills

From problem framing
to published prototypes.

This map connects the skills I applied to concrete steps and visible outcomes in both showcases. It also marks which parts are working demos and which are architecture concepts.

🧭01 · Frame

Product & business analysis

Turn user and stakeholder needs into journeys, use cases, useful questions, KPIs and prioritized next steps.

🔌02 · Connect

Data & cloud architecture

Design omnichannel ingestion, metadata enrichment, scalable warehouse flows, access boundaries and BI-ready datasets.

🌐03 · Understand

Applied AI, NLP & localization

Shape multilingual normalization, prompts, topic and sentiment taxonomies, and natural-language query experiences.

🧩04 · Model

Semantic modeling & product context

Connect customer language to shared concepts, feature maps, KPI definitions, ontology and query semantics.

✅05 · Validate

Quality, governance & BI

Use reference examples, confidence checks, privacy-aware access, validation and role-appropriate dashboards.

🛠️06 · Deliver

Frontend, UX & product delivery

Build responsive interactions, accessible navigation, browser-based DE/EN localization, visualizations and practical exports.

Implemented in these demos

Responsive HTML/CSS/JavaScript interfaces, simulated user journeys, locale-aware German/English controls, custom emoji favicons, and real CSV, Excel and PowerPoint downloads from clearly labeled sample data.

Solution architecture shown conceptually

Connections to live feedback channels, AI inference, Countly/Azure ingestion, Veezoo-to-VQL execution, Databricks and production BI are design concepts—not active integrations. No company or production data is connected.
