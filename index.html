Unique value by use case

Different problems.
Distinct advantages.

The two use cases solve different bottlenecks: turning multilingual customer comments into traceable product signals, and making governed business metrics easier to explore.

💬
01 · Feedback intelligence

From multilingual feedback volume to feature-level action

The differentiator is not just automatic sentiment labeling: it connects each customer voice to a consistent language, product feature and operating context.

  • Keep the original voice: retain source-language feedback alongside normalized translations for comparisons across markets.
  • Go beyond positive or negative: combine topic, sentiment, app feature, channel, country, platform and version context.
  • Make classifications reviewable: design for reference examples, confidence checks and downstream quality review rather than treating an AI label as ground truth.
Demo boundary: the Korean examples and classifications are illustrative; live channel ingestion and model calls are not connected.
📊
02 · Chat your data

Async self-service instead of an ad-hoc request queue

A Veezoo-based, asynchronous self-service data-exploration portal can give teams direct access to governed app-usage and feature metrics—without repeatedly queuing one-off analysis requests with partners or service providers.

  • Reduce iterative handoffs: let teams explore defined metrics asynchronously instead of sending repeated ad-hoc questions to external partners and waiting for each analysis cycle.
  • Make app and feature usage visible: use engaging, presentation-ready visualizations to review adoption and monitor usage after a feature launch.
  • Support evidence-led decisions: test product hypotheses and explore potential correlations, compare usage before and after feature releases, then carry findings into management presentations.
Architecture target—not a live integration: Veezoo/VQL, governed data access, asynchronous self-service exploration and correlation analysis are not connected or implemented in this demo. Its natural-language query and sample results are simulated; CSV, Excel and PowerPoint exports work on labeled sample data.

Feedback classification · illustrative business case

Potential for > €200,000 gross labor savings per year

At roughly 1,000 feedback items on each of 250 processing days per year, a transparent baseline scenario gives approximately €249,000/year in direct labor-cost difference versus estimated translation and LLM token costs.

Scenario, not a measured result
Manual classification · scenario baseline€250,000 / year1,000/day × 1.5 min each × €40 loaded hourly cost × 250 working days
Translation + LLM tokens · estimate provided€1,000 / yearApprox. €4 per processing day × 250 working days
Indicative gross annual difference€249,000 / yearManual labor baseline minus token estimate; before other costs
What the >€200k threshold assumes: at €40 loaded labor cost/hour and 250 working days/year, average manual handling must exceed about 1.21 minutes per feedback at 1,000 items/day. The example uses 1.5 minutes.
Validate before using as a forecast: replace handling time, fully loaded labor cost, actual processing days and token usage with measured company data. If 1,000 items are processed on 365 calendar days instead, the same assumptions yield about €363,540/year gross (€365,000 labor minus €1,460 tokens).

This is an illustrative gross direct-labor comparison, not guaranteed net savings or a complete TCO. The €4/day estimate covers token costs only; implementation, platform/infrastructure, human review, quality assurance, exceptions, monitoring and ongoing operations are excluded. Released capacity is not automatically cash savings.
